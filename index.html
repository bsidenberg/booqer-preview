What Booqer does for a service business

  • Get discovered

    A public profile that assistants that can browse the web can read: your services, service area and how to reach you.

  • Answer with approved information

    Assistants retrieve the services, price ranges and rules you approved, not guesses scraped from old listings.

  • Request or book through supported flows

    Customers can request a quote or an appointment where your rules allow it. You stay in control of what gets confirmed.

How it works

  1. Verify and configure

    We check your business and you set the services, areas and booking rules Booqer may use.

  2. Publish capabilities

    Booqer publishes a public profile and machine-readable data from what you approved.

  3. Assistants find you

    An assistant looking for a relevant local business can find your profile and read it.

  4. Approved action

    The assistant can submit an inquiry or booking request. Requests and confirmations stay distinct.

Why not an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist answers when a customer calls you. Booqer starts earlier: at the customer's own assistant, while it is still deciding who to contact. The two can work together.

You decide what Booqer may do

  • Which services and price ranges an assistant may quote.
  • Which requests need your approval before anything is confirmed.
  • Where you work and when appointments can be requested.
  • How requests reach your existing workflow, where an integration is supported.
  • Pause or revoke Booqer's authority at any time.

Questions owners ask

Can an AI assistant book me today?

Sometimes, and not everywhere. Assistants that can browse the web can find a Booqer profile and submit a request through it. Whether a given assistant does so depends on that assistant. A request is not a confirmed appointment until you approve it.

Does this replace my scheduling or field-service software?

No. Booqer sits in front of your existing tools. Where an integration is supported, requests can be passed along; it is not a replacement for the software you already use.

What does setup require?

A short application, a check of your business, and a conversation about the services, areas and rules you want published. Pilot onboarding is assisted.

Who controls pricing?

You do. Assistants only see the price ranges you approve, and anything outside your rules goes to you for approval.

How is verification handled?

We check business information, review the authority of the person claiming the business, and enforce a signed, limited delegation. Verification reduces impersonation risk; it does not vouch for service quality or legal outcomes.
