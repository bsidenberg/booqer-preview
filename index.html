Questions owners ask

Can an AI assistant book me today?

Sometimes, and not everywhere. Assistants that can browse the web can find a Booqer profile and submit a request through it. Whether a given assistant does so depends on that assistant. A request is not a confirmed appointment until you approve it.

Does this replace my scheduling or field-service software?

No. Booqer sits in front of your existing tools. Where an integration is supported, requests can be passed along; it is not a replacement for the software you already use.

What does setup require?

A short application, a check of your business, and a conversation about the services, areas and rules you want published. Pilot onboarding is assisted.

Who controls pricing?

You do. Assistants only see the price ranges you approve, and anything outside your rules goes to you for approval.

How is verification handled?

We check business information, review the authority of the person claiming the business, and enforce a signed, limited delegation. Verification reduces impersonation risk; it does not vouch for service quality or legal outcomes.
